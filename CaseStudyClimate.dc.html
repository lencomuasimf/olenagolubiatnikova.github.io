The challenge

People care about climate change. Few know what to actually do about it. Climate data feels abstract. Our job: make it Understandable → Relevant → Actionable.

Prioritising what mattered

The feature list started big. Workshop results narrowed it to the core three: assessment quiz · map · applicable data.

User testing with municipality community users shows which features attract the most attention. These insights influence our priorities and help us decide which features to focus on.

We didn’t just build a place to see climate data. We built a space that moves readers to actors.
